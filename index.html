Research

Research interests

My research focuses on psychometrics, machine learning and AI in educational data, and visualization.

01

Statistical Modeling of Innovative Item Types

Models and adaptive testing methods for assessments that capture richer response behavior, including multiple-choice, multiple-attempt items.

Examples: sequential item response models and adaptive testing for multiple-attempt items.

Multiple attemptsItem response theoryAdaptive testing
02

Machine Learning and AI in Education

Interpretable Knowledge Tracing with Measurement emphasis

Incorporating process data, curricula information, and temporal effects into knowledge-tracing models, with emphasis on interpreting how those inputs affect learning estimates.

Examples: HawkesIRT and knowledge tracing with process and curricula information.

Applications of ML/AI to quantitative methods

Using language, vision, and explainable AI to study items, response processes, and psychometric evidence.

Examples: multimodal item parameter prediction and LLM-based DIF analysis.

03

Visualization

Interactive visual analytics that help researchers understand statistical models and communicate complex findings.

Example: FAVis paper and interactive demo.
